About the camera

Active mode uses the camera to sense movement. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. All of it stays on this device.

Please clear a safe space, keep an adult nearby, and use Tap mode any time the camera is not available.

What your child learns

Counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication and division for Kinder to Grade 3, plus science about living things, the body, weather, matter and forces. Topics are guided by the DepEd curriculum for these grades.

About Project AraLo

Project AraLo is a play based learning app for Filipino preschoolers and grade schoolers. Kids learn science and math by moving their bodies, feeding Clem snacks and cheering her on.

Breast health for grown-ups

October is Breast Cancer Awareness Month. Finding changes early gives the best chance of successful treatment.

  • Know what is normal for you by looking and gently feeling your breasts regularly.
  • See a doctor about a new lump or thickening in the breast or underarm, a change in size or shape, dimpling or redness of the skin, a nipple that turns inward or leaks, or pain that does not go away.
  • Most lumps are not cancer, but every change deserves a check.
  • Ask your doctor or local health center about clinical breast exams and mammograms, often discussed from around age 40, or earlier with a family history.
  • Men can be affected too.

This is general information and not medical advice.
